---
name: write-tiny-step
description: Use when writing or reviewing a StartEase step, explanation or catalogue first action. Makes sure it is one tiny, concrete, calm action.
---
A good step:
- Is one action a person can do in a few minutes.
- Starts with a verb and names the thing to click or type.
- Is under 20 words, plain words, no jargon.
- Needs no big thinking or decision.
- Never judges or pressures.

Bad: "Plan out the flow of your project." Good: "Open diagrams.net and add one box with the word Start."
If a step has two actions, split it. If it needs a decision, make the choice for the user.
