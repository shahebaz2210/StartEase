# Tone and copy
- Short sentences, plain words, calm and warm.
- Never use: must, should, need to, have to, overdue, behind, late, failed, lazy, just, simply, easy.
- User-facing text comes from docs/copy.md or the screenshots. Do not invent text.
- A step is one concrete action that takes a few minutes.
- Confused = same step in simpler words. Too big = two smaller steps. Never add new goals.
